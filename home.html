What is ULTRAKILL?
Meet arsi "hakita" Patala
The creator of ULTRAKILL

ULTRAKILL was created by solo developer Arsi "Hakita" Patala, a Finnish game designer. He started the project independently and released the game through the publisher New Blood Interactive. Patala was inspired by classic shooters like DOOM and Quake, as well as combat style systems similar to Devil May Cry. The game's development began as an experimental project but quickly gained popularity due to its fast-paced gameplay and innovative mechanics. Released in early access in 2020, ULTRAKILL continues to receive updates and new content. Its mix of high-speed combat and retro aesthetics has earned it a dedicated fan base.

ULTRAKILL follows a blood-powered combat machine that must defeat hordes of demons to keep functioning. The game takes place in an apocalyptic setting where hell is invaded by infernal creatures. With frantic, action-packed gameplay, the goal is to survive through intense battles. The story serves as a backdrop for the fast-paced action and acrobatic combat style of the game.

HEAVEN PIERCE HER: It is a musical project by Arsi "Hakita" Patala, combining electronic and rock soundtracks with intense energy. The music complements the aggressive and retro aesthetic of his games, like ULTRAKILL, creating an immersive and dynamic experience.
